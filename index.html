Three ideas

Built for people who
just want to write.

01

Real dots,
not a font.

237 hand-built glyphs drawn dot by dot on a dim field — the way a display lights up. café, über, Kraków all render as themselves.

02

Your notes,
your disk.

Each note is a plain .rtf in a plain folder. Folders nest, notes and folders drag between them — readable by anything, forever.

03

Nothing
in the way.

No syntax, no markers, nothing to interpret. Smart quotes are off, so a # is a #. Styling lives as attributes, never markup.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

01
Real dot matrix
237 hand-built glyphs on a dim dot field. Accents, ligatures, every letter you need.
02
Instant writing
Live from the first moment. A wordmark greets an empty library and vanishes at the first keystroke.
03
Styling that stays quiet
Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, lists and per-run colour. Stored as attributes, never markup.
04
Notes are files
Plain .rtf in a plain folder. Nest, drag, rename — it's just the filesystem.
05
Search everywhere
Case- and accent-insensitive across every note, showing the line it was found on.
06
Typewriter replay
Retyped cell by cell, silently, at a chosen pace. Watch your own words land.
07
Save as Image
Export a note as a PNG drawn through the same matrix — dots, glow, colours, theme.
08
Themes & ink
System, Light, Dark; six ink colours; text size 30%–150%. Preferences persist.
09
Seven languages
EN, ES, FR, DE, PT, IT, NL — following the Mac by default.
10
Automatic saving
Debounced 0.4 s, flushed on switch, close and quit. Nothing blocks typing.
11
Essential editing
Selection, copy/paste, undo/redo, find bar (⌘F), spell check, standard shortcuts.
12
Respects your Mac
Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and VoiceOver are honoured.
Up close

Every character is a grid of light.

Not a font on a grid — the dots themselves. The face is 5 × 8 cells, hand-built: 104 plain shapes, 16 letters no composition can make, 117 accented letters composed from the plain face.

  • 237 glyphs so café, über, Kraków, naïve all light up as themselves
  • Dot field, not text the glow, the dim field, the ink — all drawn, never rendered as a font
  • Export-ready Save as Image draws through the same matrix, so the picture carries the look
